Research Objectives

DL Objective (CSR311 — Deep Learning)
  1. Utilize **LIAR** or **FakeNewsNet** datasets for training/testing.
  2. Preprocess: lowercasing, HTML removal, NLTK tokenization, 200-token padding.
  3. Architecture: **2-layer BiLSTM** (hidden=256) with Scaled Dot-Product Attention.
  4. Classification: Binary Fake/Real mapping via BCELoss + Adam optimizer.
  5. Visualization: Dynamic **Attention Heatmaps** mapping tokens to prediction drive.
  6. Reporting: Benchmarking AUC-ROC (BiLSTM+Attention vs Base).
NLP Objective (CSR322 — NLP)
  1. Fine-tune **bert-base-uncased** for semantic feature extraction.
  2. Signal Extraction: TF-IDF n-grams, POS distribution (Adj-to-Noun), Sentiment Polarity.
  3. Hybridization: Concat [CLS] embeddings with classical feature vectors (5769d).
  4. Evaluation: Cross-comparing BERT alone vs. Classical alone vs. Hybrid F1.
  5. Error Analysis: Categorizing failure modes for **20 specific misclassifications**.
  6. Discussion: Quantifying the value-add of linguistic stylometry over Transformers.
